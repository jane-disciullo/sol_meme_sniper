Six checks. Each one can only remove safety.

Every check is read-only. A token has to clear all six before the final gate will mark it safe.

Check 1

Mint and freeze authority

Confirms both authorities are disabled, and blocks when that can't be verified.

Check 2

Holder concentration

Reads Helius account data, separates pool, burn and program accounts, and keeps unknown owners in the totals.

Check 3

LP ownership and lock

Tests the liquidity pool against the project's lock rules, including minimum lock duration.

Check 4

Supply and distribution

Compares on-chain supply with holder distribution and applies concentration thresholds.

Check 5

Token-2022 transfer controls

Flags risky extensions, including unsupported or unknown ones.

Check 6

Creator and insider behavior

Looks at creator holdings, early recipients, funding links and recent outflows. A risk signal, not proof of intent.

Missing data blocks

If any check result is absent, the token is blocked and the gate names which check failed.

Demo data can't pass

Tokens from fallback or demo data are rejected no matter what the checks say.

Incomplete holder data blocks

Check 2 needs the full top 1, 5 and 10 breakdown. Partial results fail.
